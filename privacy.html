Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. No accounts

BaseMeme does not require registration. We do not ask for your name, email or password to use the tool.

2. Blockchain data is public

When you create a token, your wallet address, the token details and the transaction are recorded on the public Base blockchain. This data is public by nature and cannot be deleted by us.

3. Support chat

Our support chat is provided by Tawk.to. If you use it, the messages you send, and technical data such as your IP address, approximate location, browser and pages visited, may be collected by Tawk.to and shown to us. Do not share your seed phrase or private keys in the chat, and never will we ask for them.

4. Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and request time. The site connects to public blockchain nodes to read the fee and send your transactions.

5. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal data. We use the information above only to run the site and answer support requests.

6. Your wallet

We never see or store your seed phrase or private keys. Your wallet app handles them locally on your device.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. For questions, use the support chat on the main page.
